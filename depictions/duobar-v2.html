DuoBar (RootHide)

v2.7.2 iOS 16 · Dopamine RootHide

One circular design. Three live indicators. Battery percentage, Wi-Fi strength, and cellular signal together in a compact status-bar icon.

Design preview, not a real device screenshot. Example values are illustrative.

Features

  • Live battery percentage with an open circular charge ring
  • Wi-Fi or Personal Hotspot indicator, compact 5G/LTE label and four cellular signal dots
  • Light and dark appearances
  • Low battery, charging and Low Power Mode colors
  • Enable DuoBar switch in Settings to restore stock icons

What's New in 2.7.2

  • No more jump when Control Center closes: DuoBar lands exactly in place
  • Compact 5G/LTE label inside the ring
  • Larger sizes stay on screen on Home-button iPhones; no stock battery flash during rotation and Control Center transitions
  • Safer loading: features switch off on their own if iOS lacks a method they need

Adjustable Size

Open Settings → DuoBar → DuoBar Size to adjust the circular indicator from 70% to 140% in 5% increments. Changes apply immediately. Reset to 100% for the original size.

Compatibility

iOS 16.0–16.7.x with Dopamine RootHide and PreferenceLoader (iphoneos-arm64e), on an iOS 16 build the jailbreak supports: iPhone XS, XS Max, XR, 11 series, SE (2nd and 3rd generation), 12 series, 13 series and 14 series. Not for standard Rootless.

Not supported: iOS 17, 18 and 26; iPhone 15 and newer.

Testing

Tested on an iPhone 14 Pro Max with iOS 16.4.1 (stable, smooth Control Center closing) and in the iOS 16.4 simulator on the iPhone 12, 13 mini, 14, 14 Pro, 14 Pro Max and SE at 70%, 100% and 140%. Do not install both DuoBar (RootHide) and DuoBar (Rootless).
