DuoBar (RootHide)

v0.2.1 iOS 16 · Dopamine RootHide

One circular design. Three live indicators. Battery percentage, Wi-Fi strength, and cellular signal together in a compact status-bar icon.

Design preview, not a real device screenshot. Example values are illustrative.

Features

  • Live battery percentage with an open circular charge ring
  • Wi-Fi indicator and four cellular signal dots
  • Light and dark appearances
  • Low battery, charging and Low Power Mode colors
  • Enable DuoBar switch in Settings to restore stock icons

Updated in 0.2.1

  • Fixed duplicate Control Center battery percentage
  • Fixed rotation-lock icon overlap in Control Center
  • Added a blue Settings icon and organized preference sections
  • Added quick links to RBx9 Repo and Sileo

Compatibility

iOS 16.x, Dopamine RootHide and PreferenceLoader. Package architecture iphoneos-arm64e; not standard rootless.
