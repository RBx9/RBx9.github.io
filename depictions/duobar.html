DuoBar (RootHide)

v0.2.0 iOS 16 · RootHide

An iPhone Duo-inspired circular status bar combining battery percentage, Wi-Fi strength, and cellular signal.

Design illustration — not an actual device screenshot.

Features

  • Live battery percentage and circular charging ring
  • Wi-Fi strength and four cellular dots
  • Charging and Low Power Mode colors
  • Light and dark appearance
  • Enable or disable from Settings → DuoBar

Compatibility

Built for iOS 16.x with Dopamine RootHide. Requires tweak injection and PreferenceLoader. Not a standard rootless package.
