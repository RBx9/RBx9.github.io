DuoBar (Rootless)

v0.2.7 iOS 16 · Dopamine Rootless

One circular design. Three live indicators. Battery percentage, Wi-Fi strength, and cellular signal together in a compact status-bar icon.

Design preview, not a real device screenshot. Example values are illustrative.

Features

  • Live battery percentage with an open circular charge ring
  • Wi-Fi indicator and four cellular signal dots
  • Light and dark appearances
  • Low battery, charging and Low Power Mode colors
  • Enable DuoBar switch in Settings to restore stock icons

What's New in 0.2.7

  • Improvements to the settings experience and overall polish

Adjustable Size

Open Settings → DuoBar → DuoBar Size to adjust the circular indicator from 70% to 140% in 5% increments. Changes apply immediately. Reset to 100% for the original size.

Compatibility

iOS 16.x, Dopamine Rootless and PreferenceLoader. Package architecture iphoneos-arm64; not RootHide.

Initial Rootless build · testing pending

This v0.2.7 package has passed standard Rootless compilation, version, architecture, /var/jb installation-path and transparent-icon checks but still needs testing on a Rootless iPhone. Do not install both RootHide and Rootless variants at once.
